# Privacy Policy

**Tasbih – Dhikr Counter**

**Last Updated:** November 23, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to **Tasbih – Dhikr Counter**.

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information the Tasbih application stores, how it is used, and how you can manage or delete it.

Tasbih is designed to provide a simple and private dhikr-counting experience while keeping your personal app data on your device.

## 2. Information Stored by the App

Tasbih stores the following information locally on your device:

- **Your Name:** The name you enter when opening the app for the first time, used to personalize your experience.
- **Dhikr Preferences:** Your selected dhikr and counting preferences.
- **Counter Information:** Your current count and daily progress.
- **Daily Goals:** Your personal dhikr targets.
- **App Settings:** Your selected appearance, sounds and other preferences.
- **Dhikr History:** A record of how many times you have recited each dhikr on different days.

This information is stored on your device and is not transmitted to the developer.

## 3. How Your Information Is Used

The information stored by the application is used only to provide its features, including:

- Personalizing your experience with your name.
- Remembering your selected dhikr.
- Maintaining your current counting progress.
- Tracking your daily goals.
- Saving your dhikr history.
- Remembering your preferred app settings.

## 4. Data Collection and Sharing

Tasbih does not collect, transmit, sell or share your personal information.

The application does not include user accounts, advertisements or analytics services.

Your information remains stored locally on your device and is used only for the application's functionality.

## 5. Data Backup

If Android's backup feature is enabled on your device, application data may be included in your personal Google backup so that it can be restored when setting up a new device.

Such backups are managed by Android and your Google account and are subject to Google's applicable policies.

## 6. Data Deletion

You can manage and delete your information whenever you wish.

To clear your dhikr history:

**Open the app → Settings → Clear All Dhikr History**

You can also uninstall the application to remove its locally stored data from your device.

Please note that data included in an existing Android or Google backup may need to be deleted separately.

## 7. Children's Privacy

Tasbih does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

The application does not require users to create an account or submit personal information to use its features.

## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when necessary to reflect changes to the application or its features.

Any updates will be published on this page along with a revised update date.

We encourage users to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
