# Privacy Policy — Tasbih

_Last updated: [DATE]_

Tasbih ("the app") is a dhikr counter. **It does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.**

**What the app stores, on your phone only**
- The name you type on first launch (used to greet you)
- Your chosen dhikr, count, daily goal, look and sound settings
- How many times you said each dhikr on each day (your history)

This information never leaves your phone through the app. The app has no internet permission, no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party SDKs.

**Backups**
If you have Android's backup turned on, Android may include the app's data in your own Google backup so it can be restored on a new phone. That backup is handled by Android and your Google account, not by the app or its developer.

**Deleting your data**
Open Settings → "Clear all dhikr history", or uninstall the app. Both remove the data from your phone.

**Children**
The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
