## Full description (max 4000)
Tasbih is a calm, beautiful dhikr counter that feels like holding real prayer beads.

SLIDE THE BEADS
Slide a bead up with your thumb, or just tap. Each bead glides along the string and gives a soft, natural click and a gentle vibration — never a game-like beep. Bigger beads mark every 33, just like a real tasbih.

YOUR DAILY DHIKR, AT A GLANCE
Every day's dhikr is saved on your phone. Open Progress to see how much you said today or on any recent day, what you recited, your daily-goal ring, consistent days in a row, your best day and your all-time total.

GLAD TIDINGS FROM THE SUNNAH
See hadith-based virtues of dhikr (from Sahih al-Bukhari and Sahih Muslim) with your progress shown on each one — for example 100 × SubhanAllahi wa bihamdihi, the dhikr after prayer, and the dhikr before sleep. Only Allah knows the true reward; the app is there to help you stay consistent.

BUILT-IN DHIKR
• After every prayer (33 · 33 · 33 · La ilaha illallah)
• Tasbih of Fatima before sleep (33 · 33 · 34)
• SubhanAllah, Alhamdulillah, Allahu Akbar
• La ilaha illallah, Astaghfirullah, Durood
• La hawla wa la quwwata illa billah
• SubhanAllahi wa bihamdihi, SubhanAllahil Azim
Choose your own target (33, 99, 100, 500, 1000) or count freely.

MADE TO BE BEAUTIFUL
• Four looks: Emerald Ivory, Rose Pearl, Midnight Jade and Sandalwood
• A mihrab-style arch and a floating navigation bar that match your look
• Quiet sound options: Soft click, Wooden, or Silent — with its own volume
• Greets you by name, with the Islamic date

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Works 100% offline — the app has no internet permission
• No ads, no accounts, no tracking
• Your dhikr stays on your phone

May Allah accept from you.
