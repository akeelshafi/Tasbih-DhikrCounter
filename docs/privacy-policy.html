Privacy Policy

Tasbih Dhikr Counter

Last Updated: November 23, 2026

Thank you for using Tasbih Dhikr Counter. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app stores, how it is used, and how you can manage or delete it.

1. Information Stored by the App

Tasbih Dhikr Counter is designed to work without requiring users to create an account or provide personal information to use its main features.

The app may store the following information locally on your device:

  • Your name, if you choose to enter it.
  • Your selected Dhikr preferences.
  • Your Tasbih counting progress and counts.
  • Your daily Dhikr goals.
  • Your daily Dhikr history.
  • Your app preferences, such as appearance and sound settings.

This information is used to provide the app's features and maintain your progress and preferences.

2. How Your Information Is Used

Information stored by the app is used only to provide and improve your experience within Tasbih Dhikr Counter, including:

  • Saving your counting progress.
  • Maintaining your Dhikr history.
  • Remembering your selected preferences.
  • Displaying your daily goals and progress.

3. Data Collection and Sharing

Tasbih Dhikr Counter does not collect or transmit your locally stored app information to the developer or any third party.

The app does not use advertising or analytics services. Your locally stored information is not sold, rented, or shared by the app.

4. Data Backup

Depending on your Android device settings and backup configuration, some app data may be included in Android device backups. Such backups are managed by Android and your device or account settings.

5. Data Deletion

You can manage your stored information through the app's available settings. You can clear your Dhikr history using the Clear All Dhikr History option in Settings.

Uninstalling the app generally removes its locally stored data from your device. However, data included in an Android backup may remain in that backup according to your device settings.

6. Children's Privacy

Tasbih Dhikr Counter does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app's locally stored information is used only for its intended functionality.
